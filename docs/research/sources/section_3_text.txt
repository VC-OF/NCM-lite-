3 Next-Concept Modeling NCM generates text through a learned coarse-to-fine hierarchy of discrete codes. We first train a multiscale residual VQ tokenizer to represent text at progressively finer levels of detail, then freeze it and train a transformer to generate the hierarchy. At each level, the transformer predicts all codes in parallel, conditioned on the cumulative reconstruction of earlier levels. The tokenizer’s decoder maps the completed reconstruction back to text.
